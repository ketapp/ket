# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Be constructive, assume good intent, accept correction gracefully, and focus
criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is harmful. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, consistent, and protect the privacy of reporters.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact described in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project elsewhere.
